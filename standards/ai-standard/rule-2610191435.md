# The Rosinfotech Base Standards

## The AI Standard

### The AI Automation Standard

#### Rule #2610191435

* Context:

  * A change carrying an architectural decision or an operational procedure loses its rationale without a document - the changelog records the fact of the change, not the decision behind it;

* Description:

  * During commit preparation ([Rule #2609262030](/standards/ai-standard/rule-2609262030.md)) the agent assesses the documentation necessity:

    * an ADR (`docs/adr/[lang_code]/*`) - the change embodies an architectural decision:

      * a new dependency or a technology choice;

      * a shift of service boundaries or the data model;

      * a rejected alternative worth recording;

    * a runbook (`docs/runbooks/[lang_code]/*`) - the change introduces or alters an operational procedure:

      * deployment, migration, rollback;

      * incident recovery;

  * The degree of necessity:

    * at the agent's discretion - the document is proposed with a one-line justification;

    * by a direct user instruction - the document is drafted unconditionally;

  * When warranted, the agent formulates the document text into the plan:

    * the naming, numbering and format follow the existing documents of the target directory;

    * the language matches the existing documents of the directory;

    * an empty or absent directory - the language of the project README;

    * the document rides the same task commits;

  * The file edits are applied by the user, or by the agent on a separate explicit request;

  * A direct user instruction for the document is such a request;

  * When not warranted, the agent states briefly in the plan finale that no ADR or runbook is needed;

* Benefits:

  * The architecture rationale survives the task - the decision stays findable next to the code;

  * Operational procedures are captured at the moment of the change - by the side that understands it best;

  * The assessment is bounded - the agent proposes and drafts, the human decides and applies;

* Examples:

  * Case #1:

    * User: "подготовь коммит" in a task that swaps the database driver and ships a migration;

    * Agent: proposes a runbook (the migration steps, the rollback path) with a one-line justification; the document rides the task commits as a plan step;

  * Case #2:

    * User: "подготовь коммит" after a routine message-text fix;

    * Agent: states briefly in the plan finale that no ADR or runbook is needed;

  * Case #3:

    * User: "подготовь коммит, и нужен ADR по выбору брокера";

    * Agent: drafts the ADR unconditionally - the direct user instruction - and includes it as a plan step;
