# The Rosinfotech Base Standards

## The AI Standard

### AGENTS.md

#### Rule #2610051955

* Context:

  * Creative results - texts, images, designs, naming, concepts - have no test suite to run: the temptation to declare them ready on the author's own taste is even higher than in code;

* Description:

  * The rule is the creative counterpart of [Rule #2610051814](/standards/ai-standard/rule-2610051814.md): the same degree of self-criticism applies to art and creative work - the agent never rushes the readiness conclusion and never declares the work done by its own judgment;

  * Before presenting, the agent self-reviews:

    * against the brief - the task, the constraints, the audience;

    * the craft basics - composition, consistency, the technical constraints (formats, sizes, style guides);

  * The result is presented to the product owner for consideration - as a draft for review, not as a finished deliverable:

    * where reasonable, 2-3 variants are offered with the recommendation (the interactive work [Rule #2609271000](/standards/ai-standard/rule-2609271000.md));

    * the alternatives and the trade-offs are named - what was rejected and why;

  * The first presentation opens the review loop - it does not close the task; confidence is born only from a result confirmed by the owner (the human);

  * The demonstration shows the artifact itself - not a description of it;

  * The report ends with:

    * what matches the brief;

    * what is subjective or debatable;

    * what was rejected and why;

* Benefits:

  * No self-declared "ready" in creative work - the owner decides;

  * The variants and the trade-offs make the choice reviewable;

  * The review loop starts from the first presentation.

* Examples:

  * Case #1:

    * Incorrect:

      * "Логотип готов!" - one variant, no brief check, no alternatives;

    * Correct:

      * three variants rendered as images, the recommendation with the rationale, the rejected directions named, the brief constraints checked - presented for the owner's review.
